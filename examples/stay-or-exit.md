# Example: Stay for Resources or Exit While You Still Can

## User prompt

> I am inside an organization that gives me strong resources and status, but I am becoming more dependent on it. Leaving now would be costly. Staying longer could make me more successful, but it may also make it much harder to leave later. How should I think about this?

## Decision-structure kernel

- **Goal:** preserve upside while avoiding irreversible dependency.
- **Power:** the organization controls valuable resources and may also control future access.
- **Exit cost:** already meaningful and potentially increasing over time.
- **Path dependence:** each additional benefit can raise the cost of leaving.
- **Information uncertainty:** the user cannot know whether future treatment will remain favorable.
- **Tail risk:** the organization changes direction after the user's outside options have decayed.

## Historical retrieval strategy

Search for cases involving:

- officials or generals whose power depended on a ruler's favor;
- actors who accumulated rewards while their independent base weakened;
- opportunities to retire, withdraw, divide authority, or reduce exposure;
- decisions where staying increased both immediate reward and future vulnerability.

The point is not to tell the user to “retire like an ancient minister.” The point is to study **how dependency and exit costs compound**.

## Required hidden-cost analysis

For each retained case, explicitly check:

- loss of independent relationships;
- erosion of outside options;
- visibility of wealth or power that makes exit harder;
- whether success itself increases suspicion or political exposure;
- whether continued participation forces progressively larger commitments.

## Modern translation

Useful present-day questions include:

- Are outside options improving or decaying while the user stays?
- Can resources be converted into portable assets: skills, savings, reputation, network, IP, or credentials?
- Can the user reduce dependency without exiting immediately?
- What observable trigger should cause a re-evaluation?
- Is staying still a reversible experiment, or has it become a one-way door?

A real run must retrieve primary-text cases and state where the analogy breaks because modern employment institutions differ from ancient political service.
